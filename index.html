FAQ

Questions worth asking

Do you come to me?

Yes — we work at your home or your workplace. We just need somewhere safe and legal to park with room to work around the car. If there's no outdoor tap or outlet, say so when you book and we'll bring what we need.

How long does it take?

An express wash is under an hour. An interior or exterior detail is usually two to three. A full detail on a car that hasn't been done in a while can be most of a day — we'd rather tell you that up front than rush it.

Will it fix scratches?

Paint correction removes swirls and light scratches properly. Deep ones that catch a fingernail have gone through the clear coat and can't be polished out — we'll tell you which yours are before you pay for anything.

What if it rains?

We'll reschedule, free, as many times as the weather needs. Nobody gets charged for a forecast.

How do I pay?

When the detail is finished and you've looked over the car. No deposit to book.

Do I need to empty the car?

Please do — glovebox, boot and door pockets included. It means we can clean everywhere, and it keeps your things your responsibility rather than ours.
