Choosing

Which detail does my car need?

Pick by how long it has been and what is bothering you. If the car is tidy but dull, an express wash keeps it that way. If it is lived in, start inside. If the paint looks hazy in sunlight, that is correction, not washing.

What's included

What does a full detail actually include?

A full detail covers the whole interior and the whole exterior in one visit. Inside: vacuum, steam, shampoo, leather and plastics. Outside: a two-bucket hand wash, decontamination, wheels and arches, trim, and a sealant so it stays clean longer.

Inside the car

  • Full vacuum — seats, rails, boot, under the mats
  • Steam and shampoo on carpets and upholstery
  • Leather cleaned and conditioned, not greased
  • Plastics and vents detailed, dressed matte
  • Glass inside and out, streak-free

Outside the car

  • Two-bucket hand wash — no brushes, no swirls
  • Wheels, barrels and arches cleaned properly
  • Decontamination for tar and embedded fallout
  • Trim and tyres restored and dressed
  • Sealant or wax so it stays clean longer

FAQ

Car detailing questions, answered

What is car detailing?

Car detailing is a deep clean and restoration of a vehicle inside and out, done by hand rather than by machine. It covers vacuuming, shampooing and conditioning the interior, and washing, decontaminating, polishing and protecting the paint outside. A wash cleans a car; a detail restores it.

How long does car detailing take?

An express wash takes under an hour. A single interior or exterior detail usually takes two to three hours. A full detail on a car that hasn't been cleaned in a long time can take most of a day. You're told the realistic time when you're quoted, not after the work starts.

Do you come to me?

Yes. Banana Detail is mobile and works at your home or workplace. All that's needed is somewhere safe and legal to park with room to work around the car. If there's no outdoor tap or power outlet available, say so when booking and the equipment is brought along.

Does detailing remove scratches?

Paint correction removes swirl marks and light scratches by machine polishing the clear coat, rather than filling them temporarily. Deep scratches that catch a fingernail have gone through the clear coat and can't be polished out. You're told which type yours are before paying for anything.

How often should a car be detailed?

A full detail twice a year suits most daily drivers, with an express wash and wax every four to six weeks in between. Cars kept outside, driven through winter salt, or carrying pets and children benefit from more frequent interior work.

What happens if it rains?

The appointment is rescheduled free of charge, as many times as the weather requires. Nobody is charged a cancellation fee because of a forecast.

Do I need to empty the car first?

Yes — glovebox, boot and door pockets included. It means every surface can be cleaned properly, and your belongings stay your responsibility rather than the detailer's.

How do I pay?

When the detail is finished and you've looked over the car. There's no deposit to book, and the price was agreed before any work started.
